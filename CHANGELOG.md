## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added

- SDL2 graphical CHIP-8 frontend and original animated demo ROM.
- SDL-independent C11 core, deterministic random source and configurable quirk profiles.
- Baseline CHIP-8 instructions, loader validation, headless tracing and configurable CPU scheduler.
- Optional SDL square-wave buzzer driven by `sound_timer`.
- Unit tests, sanitizer target and cross-platform headless CI workflow.
- SDL keyboard-mapping test, configurable CPU rate and optional SDL buzzer.
- Apache-2.0 asset metadata, dependency/distribution policy and community health files.
- Compatibility matrix and manual runner for independently obtained external conformance ROMs.
